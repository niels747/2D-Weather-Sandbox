A fixed, rotationally symmetric atmosphere: every radius is a ring. No moving or multiple-vortex tornadoes. Cloud shape comes from simulated moisture; a funnel is not guaranteed.

Chamber

The vortex preset supplies rotating air and central lift. The flow then evolves freely under those forces.

Forcing

Controls change the forcing and outer air reservoir. Use Reset to apply them as fresh starting conditions.

Interact

Drag to paint a ring. Hold Shift to subtract. Both visible halves represent the same air.

Normal 2D and this chamber keep separate sessions.

Model & diagnostics

Annular finite volumes; radial/vertical pressure projection; transported angular momentum; centrifugal acceleration; surface drag; simplified moist buoyancy and saturation adjustment.

Constant reference density, flat ground, a closed ceiling and an open outer radius. Pressure is a perturbation, not sea-level pressure. Radiation, terrain, hail and the original precipitation particles are not included in this experimental mode.
